ABOUT PINDROP

A simpler way to save inspiration.

Sometimes you want to keep a video for offline reference, personal inspiration, or a project you are authorized to use. PinDrop provides a straightforward interface for supported, publicly accessible Pinterest media.

Availability depends on the Pin, Pinterest's current access rules, and the media format. Private, deleted, or unsupported Pins may not work.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about saving Pinterest media.

How do I download a Pinterest video?+

Copy the link of a public Pin, paste it into the downloader, and click Download. If the Pin is supported, a preview and download option will appear.

Can I download images and GIFs too?+

This starter version focuses on media supported by the installed extractor. Image and GIF support depends on the Pin and may need additional implementation.

Why is my link not working?+

The Pin may be private, deleted, restricted, or unsupported, or Pinterest may have changed how its media is delivered. Try another public Pin.

Can I download private Pins?+

No. PinDrop does not bypass login requirements, privacy controls, or access restrictions.

Is PinDrop affiliated with Pinterest?+

No. PinDrop is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest. Respect copyright and only save or reuse media when you have the necessary rights.
